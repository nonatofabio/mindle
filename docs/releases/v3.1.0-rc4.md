## What's new

### Walking through changes with the banner

Multi-chunk diffs used to force you to scroll the reader by hand to find each pending change. The track-changes banner now exposes **◀ Previous / Next ▶** arrows and a position counter — `N of M pending changes` once you step into a chunk, the plain `M pending changes` before that. The chunk you land on gets a brief accent ring so the eye catches the new target. Arrows disable at the boundaries so the chunk list's ends are visible.

Closes [#38](https://github.com/nonatofabio/mindle/issues/38).

### Viewport stays put when you accept or reject

Accepting a per-chunk change used to drift the document — the captured scroll position pointed at the chunk's old document-Y, but the doc collapsed above it and that Y no longer pointed at the same content. The viewport jumped, the accepted text scrolled off-screen, and you had to find your place again.

The accept / reject handlers now capture two things at click time: the chunk wrapper's viewport-y (where it sat on screen) and a snippet of plain readable text from the replacement. After the round-trip render, the reader finds the snippet in the new DOM and adjusts scroll so the same content lands at the same screen position. The change stays put under your cursor; everything else reflows around it.

Closes [#39](https://github.com/nonatofabio/mindle/issues/39).

Robustness notes for nerds:
- The snippet picker takes the longest run of plain text from the replacement, dropping out at markdown decoration (pipes, brackets, fences, list markers) so it actually round-trips to the rendered DOM.
- When the snippet appears multiple times in the doc, the match closest to where the chunk lived wins — the first occurrence isn't always the right one.
- A safety gate falls back to your pre-action scroll position if the chosen match is more than two viewport-heights away from where you clicked, so a homograph elsewhere in a long doc can't yank the viewport.
